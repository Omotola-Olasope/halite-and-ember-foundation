About us

A long-term commitment to human potential.

The Halite & Ember Foundation is governed from England and Wales, with Nigeria as its first operating country. It is Christian in origin, ethics and governance, and universal in service.

Our mission

Our objects

The objects of the Foundation are, for the public benefit:

  1. The advancement of education,

    in particular but not exclusively among persons whose access to education is restricted by financial hardship or disadvantage, by means including:

    1. the provision of scholarships, bursaries and grants covering tuition fees, examination fees, books, equipment, technology and learning materials;
    2. the provision of tuition, mentoring, coaching, careers guidance and wellbeing support; and
    3. support for academic, vocational, technical, digital and professional education, training and qualifications.

  2. The prevention and relief of poverty and the relief of unemployment,

    in particular but not exclusively among persons whose economic opportunity is restricted by financial hardship or disadvantage, by means including:

    1. the provision of education, training, work experience, business skills development, mentoring, advice and guidance; and
    2. the provision of grants, goods, services and facilities to help such persons obtain employment, or establish or develop a trade, business or social enterprise;

    in each case as a means of relieving the poverty or unemployment of the persons assisted.

Our principles

How we work.

  1. We invest in people, not projects.

  2. We are stewards, not owners.

  3. We are governed by conviction and we serve without condition.

  4. We work to a standard worthy of the people we serve.

Faith and service

Christian in origin. Universal in service.

The Foundation is Christian in origin, ethics and governance, and universal in service. Its Christian character shapes who governs it and the standards to which it holds itself.

Faith determines who governs. It never determines who benefits. No beneficiary is selected, preferred, excluded or assessed by religion, and no condition of belief attaches to any support.

We serve people of every faith and of none, on the same terms.

Our standard

Stewardship, accountability, duration and quality.

Stewardship
We hold capital on behalf of people not yet born, and we publish our accounts whether or not the law requires it.
Accountability
Every funding decision has a written file recording its reasoning, its conditions and its outcome.
Duration
We commit for the length of a programme and the relationship around it, not for a single payment.
Quality
We work to a standard worthy of the people we serve, in what we fund and in how we fund it.

The Halite & Ember Foundation exists to invest in people whose potential exceeds their opportunity.
