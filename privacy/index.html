Privacy notice

How we handle your information

This notice explains what we collect when you apply to the Halite & Ember Foundation Bursary, what we do with it, who sees it, and how to get it removed. It is written to be read, not to protect us.

Last updated 27 September 2026

We will never ask you for money or for your bank details. We do not pay money to applicants. If you are selected, we pay ALX directly. If anyone contacts you claiming to represent this Foundation and asks you for a payment, a fee, or your bank or card details, it is not us. Please tell us at hello@haliteandemberfoundation.org.

Who is responsible for your information

The Halite & Ember Foundation is being established as a charitable incorporated organisation in England and Wales. That registration is not yet complete, so the Foundation is not yet a separate legal body, and this round is funded personally by its founder, who is the person responsible in law for the information you give us.

Your application is never shared with any organisation other than those listed under “Who sees it” below.

You can reach us about anything in this notice at hello@haliteandemberfoundation.org. It is the only address we use. We will reply within thirty days.

What we collect

Everything we hold about you comes from the application form, or from you directly if you write to us. We do not buy information about you and we do not collect it from anywhere else.

What the form asks for

  • Your full name.
  • Your date of birth, and which age band you fall into on 14 August 2026.
  • Which Lagos local government area you live in.
  • Your phone number, including your WhatsApp number if it differs. This is our main way of contacting you.
  • Your email address, if you choose to give one. This is optional.
  • Whether you have already been awarded a place on another programme we funded this year.
  • Which ALX pathway you want to take, and whether you already have an ALX account.
  • What device you will use, and whether you can commit the hours the programme asks for.
  • Your answers to three written questions, including what has made paying the monthly fee yourself difficult.
  • How you heard about the bursary.
  • Your confirmation that your answers are true and that you agree to us using them to decide this bursary.

If you would rather speak than write, you can send a voice note by WhatsApp instead of typing your answers. If you do, we hold that recording alongside your application and delete it at the same time.

What we do not ask, and never take into account

We do not ask about your religion, and we never select, prefer, exclude or assess anyone by reference to it. That is written into the Foundation's Founding Charter and it is not a matter of policy that can be quietly changed. One of the options for how you heard about us mentions religious organisations; that tells us where to advertise the next round and nothing more, and it plays no part in any decision.

We also do not ask for your exam results, your employment history, references, or who you know.

Why we use it, and our legal basis for doing so

  • To decide who receives a bursary. We rely on your consent, which you give with the confirmation box at the end of the form. You can withdraw it at any time, though if you withdraw it before we decide, we cannot consider your application.
  • To contact you about your application. Every applicant gets a reply, successful or not. We use your phone number, or your email address if you gave one.
  • To arrange the programme for people we select. We use your name, contact details and pathway choice to enrol you with ALX.
  • To keep an honest record of how we decided. The Founding Charter binds the Foundation to record the reasoning and outcome of every funding decision it makes. We rely on our legitimate interest in being accountable for money we have given away.
  • To prevent fraud and impersonation. People pretending to represent funders is a real problem, and we rely on our legitimate interest in protecting applicants from it.

Who sees it

  • The founder and the Selection Lead. Nobody else at the Foundation sees applications.
  • ALX, but only if you are selected, and only the details needed to enrol you. Applications that are not selected are never sent to ALX.
  • Google, which provides the form, the spreadsheet your answers are stored in, and our email. Google processes the information on our instructions and not for its own purposes.
  • WhatsApp, if you choose to send a voice note.

We do not sell your information. We do not share it for advertising. We do not publish it. If we ever want to tell your story publicly, we will ask you first, by name, and you are free to say no. The Charter forbids us from presenting the people we support in a way that trades their dignity for our reputation.

Where your information is held

You are applying from Nigeria and we are based in the United Kingdom, so your information travels between the two. It is stored on Google's infrastructure, which may hold it in the European Union, the United Kingdom or the United States. We rely on the transfer arrangements the United Kingdom recognises for this purpose, together with the contract terms Google offers its customers.

We are subject to the United Kingdom's data protection law. Because you are in Nigeria, the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 also applies to this processing, and this notice is written to meet both.

How long we keep it

  • If you are not selected, we delete your application within six months of this round closing, so by 14 February 2027 at the latest.
  • If you are selected, we keep your application for the three months of your programme and then for six years, because we have to be able to account for money we have spent.
  • The record of the decision itself is kept permanently, as the Charter requires. We reduce it to the decision, the reasoning and the outcome, and we do not keep your full application or your contact details in it.
  • Voice notes are deleted at the same time as the written application they belong to.
  • If you are under 18, the form still lets you submit. We will not use your application to decide this bursary, we will delete it within thirty days, and we will not keep your details to contact you about future rounds.

Your rights

You can ask us to do any of the following, free of charge, and we will do it within thirty days.

  • Tell you what we hold about you, and give you a copy.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete what we hold. If you ask before the decision, we cannot consider your application.
  • Stop using it, or restrict what we do with it.
  • Withdraw the consent you gave on the form.
  • Give you your answers in a portable form, so you can reuse them elsewhere.

Write to hello@haliteandemberfoundation.org and say what you want. You do not need to explain why, and asking will never affect how your application is judged.

There is no automated decision making. Every application is read by a person.

If you are unhappy with us

Tell us first, at hello@haliteandemberfoundation.org, and we will try to put it right. You do not have to come to us first, though, and you can complain directly to a regulator.

In Nigeria, that is the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. In the United Kingdom, it is the Information Commissioner's Office, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. If the change is significant and affects an application we already hold, we will contact you about it.

When the Foundation completes its charity registration, legal responsibility for your information passes from the founder to the registered organisation. We will update this page and say so plainly when that happens.
