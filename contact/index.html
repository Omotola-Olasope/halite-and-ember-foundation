A note on safety

The Foundation will never ask you for money, a fee, or your bank or card details. If anyone claiming to represent the Foundation asks you for a payment, it is not us. Please tell us at hello@haliteandemberfoundation.org.

To learn how we handle the information you share with us, read our privacy notice.
