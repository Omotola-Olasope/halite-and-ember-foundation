The Halite & Ember Foundation

The Halite & Ember Foundation exists to invest in people whose potential exceeds their opportunity.

We work across education, enterprise, the arts and youth development, supporting people whose gifts have outrun the opportunities available to them. Every commitment we make carries relationship and duration as well as money, because we invest in people rather than projects. The Foundation is Christian in its governance and universal in its service: it serves people of all faiths, and asks those it serves to subscribe to nothing.

The Foundation is currently being established.
