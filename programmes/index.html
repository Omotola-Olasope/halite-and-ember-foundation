Our approach

Funding carries relationship, accountability and duration.

  • Relationship

    People, not payments.

    Money never travels alone. Every award comes with people who know those we support by name.

  • Accountability

    Criteria set down in writing.

    Selection follows written criteria, and every funding decision has a written file.

  • Duration

    Measured in months and years.

    Support is sized to the length of a programme, not to a single grant.
