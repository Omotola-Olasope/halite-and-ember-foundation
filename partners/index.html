Partnership with purpose.

We work through partners who deliver excellent education and training, so that our funding reaches people through programmes of real quality.

Delivery partner

ALX Africa

ALX Africa trains young people across the African continent in technology and professional skills.

It is the Foundation's delivery partner for both of our current programmes, the Data Analytics Scholarship and the Foundation Bursary.
